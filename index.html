Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the flight from the UK to Dubai?

It's a manageable long-haul journey, short enough that many travellers fly overnight and land refreshed thanks to flat-bed business class seating.

Is lounge access always included?

Most business class fares to Dubai include full lounge access. Our team confirms exactly what's included before you book, so there are no surprises on the day.

Can I stop over in Dubai on a longer journey?

Yes. Dubai is a popular stopover point, and we can help build a multi-city itinerary if Dubai is one leg of a longer trip.

What's the difference between business and premium class here?

Business class flights to Dubai include flat-bed seating and full lounge access, while premium class flights to Dubai offer extra comfort at a lower price point.

How far ahead should I book?

A few months ahead is ideal, especially around UK school holidays and major events in Dubai, when premium cabins sell out fastest.
